TERRAIN of the whole map, one character per cell of S x S pixels (S given below); column c covers x = c*S .. c*S+S-1, row r is y = r*S (a standing character's y is the row of the floor it stands on). _ floor you can reach from where you are now; x floor you CANNOT reach from here; # solid ground/wall (not passable); w wood/rock obstacle (not passable, jump over); . open air (passable, you fall through it); @ you; E exit to another map; N NPC; M monster; i item on the ground; P another player.
